Bruce Lee's usual order

Bruce Lee was a regular at Tai Tung. He often sat at the same table in the back and ordered oyster sauce beef and garlic shrimp.

Oyster Sauce Beef

I'd order it with rice so I could scoop up the sauce and onions together.

What I'd order with friends

I'd start with fried chicken wings, then add beef chow fun so everyone can try the wide rice noodles. I'd get sweet and sour pork as a third dish for anyone who likes a sweeter sauce.
