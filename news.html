History & News

From Grandpa Quan's first restaurant to the return of the neon sign.

The story of Tai Tung

  1. 1935

    Grandpa Quan opens Tai Tung

    Tai Tung became Seattle's oldest Chinese restaurant still in operation. Grandpa Quan's family has kept the restaurant going across generations.

  2. 1960

    The original sign comes down

    The restaurant removed its neon Chop Suey sign during a remodel.

  3. 2018

    The neon sign returns

    A replica went up outside the restaurant, bringing back a piece of the original storefront.
